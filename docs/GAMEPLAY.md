# Gameplay reference

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Touch: press anywhere and drag left / right | steer (floating joystick, only the horizontal axis is used) |
| Keyboard `A` / `D` or arrow keys (editor / desktop) | steer |
| Tap anywhere or **PLAY** on the main menu | start the run |
| Pause button, `Esc` (Android back button), leaving the app | pause |

The blob runs forward on its own. Speed rises with the level (4.0 m/s on level 1 up to 5.5 m/s from level 11).

## Body parts and cut cascades

The blob has 11 parts. A hit on a part also takes off everything that hangs on it ("cascade"). The data lives in `Player.prefab` (`BodyPart.relatedBodyParts`).

| Hit part | Parts that come off | Count |
|---|---|---|
| Head | Head | 1 |
| Torso upper | Torso upper, both arms (upper + lower), Head | 6 |
| Torso lower | Torso lower, both legs (upper + lower) | 5 |
| Arm upper (L / R) | that arm (upper + lower) | 2 |
| Arm lower (L / R) | the lower arm | 1 |
| Leg upper (L / R) | that leg (upper + lower) | 2 |
| Leg lower (L / R) | the lower leg | 1 |

* The blob is **lost** when all 11 parts are gone (e.g. a hip bar *and* a chest bar before the next loot).
* Cut pieces gather in an invisible `JellyContainer`, wobble, slide forward a little and shrink away shortly before they would leave the ray marching volume.
* **Locomotion** follows the legs: both upper legs present → run; one missing → hop on the other leg; both missing → crawl. (`PController.RefreshLocomotion`, driven by `Player.PartsChanged`.)

## Obstacles

Horizontal bars of fixed thickness. Their height decides what they cut:

| Bar height | Cuts | Parts (upright blob) |
|---|---|---|
| 0.40 | shins | 2 |
| 0.74 | thighs (+ cascade) | 4 |
| 0.99 | hips (torso lower + cascade) | 5 |
| 1.30 | chest (torso upper + cascade) | 6 |
| 1.64 | head | 1 |

Kinds: **static bar** (3.5 m long, enters the lane from one side – dodge by steering to the open side), **wall** (spans the whole lane, cannot be dodged), **needle** (two bars leaving a 1.0 – 1.5 m gap), **sliding bar** (sweeps across the lane, from level 4).

## Loot

A wobbling jelly ball that **regrows every cut off part** in the loot's colour (so a recovering blob turns multi-coloured). Parts fly in from the loot, grow back with an overshoot and are harmless (no colliders) until they have arrived.

## Levels

* **Level 1** keeps the hand authored obstacles and loot of `Scene_001` (finish at Z = 120).
* **Level ≥ 2** is generated by `LevelGenerator` (deterministic: the same level number always gives the same track). The authored objects are cloned as templates.
* A level number also picks a colour theme (six themes, cycling).

Fairness rules, enforced by unit tests for levels 1 – 80:

* every row that is not a wall or a sliding bar leaves at least 1.0 m of free lane;
* walls are limited per level (none on level 1, then `1 + level/3`, max 6) and are always "paid for": loot within 56 m before and 20 m after;
* loot is inside the lane, never overlaps a bar and is spaced at least 4 m apart;
* rows are at least 9 m apart and the last 22 m before the finish are clear.

### Difficulty curve

Each level is generated as 24 candidates; a Monte-Carlo model of the cut / regrow rules (`DifficultyModel`) estimates how often an *average* player (dodges 75 % of dodgeable bars, collects 92 % of the loot) would lose, and the candidate closest to the target curve is kept. This keeps difficulty smooth instead of depending on the luck of a single random layout.

## Scoring

```
score = 500 + 100·level            base
      + round(1000 · integrity)    integrity = attached parts / 11 at the finish
      + 100 · loot collected
      + min(1000, 20 · seconds saved against the par time)    par = track length / speed · 1.1
stars: ≥ 70 % integrity → 3, ≥ 35 % → 2, otherwise 1 (finishing always gives at least one)
```

## Save data

Stored as JSON in `PlayerPrefs` (key `blobrunner.save.v1`, class `SaveData`): current level, highest level, best score, total score / stars, loot, runs / wins, the sound / music / vibration switches and whether the steering hint was dismissed. Values are sanitised on load (hand edited or corrupted saves can not break the game) and written when a level ends, when a switch changes, when the app is paused and on quit.
